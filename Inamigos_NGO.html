02

Get to Know Us Better

Founded 23 Sept 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos is a Section 8, 80G & 12A registered non-profit based in Chhattisgarh — CSR-1 and NITI Aayog registered, ISO 9001:2015 certified. Our work spans food & clothing drives, education, animal welfare, women's empowerment and environmental sustainability.

About / Trust Section
Certifications (80G, 12A, CSR-1) shown near a real photo build donor trust faster than text alone.
